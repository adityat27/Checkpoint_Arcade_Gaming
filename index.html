Bam! Brawl

  • Both players lock in a move at the same time, then it all explodes. Keys 1–6 pick a move, Enter locks it in.
  • Strike beats Grab · Grab beats Guard · Guard blocks Strike · Kick cracks through Guard.
  • Grab also steals 1⚡. Mega needs 3⚡ and hits for 3. You can't Guard twice in a row.
  • Empty your rival's 6 health segments to win.

Glaze Craze

  • Both bakers get the same order ticket and 75 seconds to copy it.
  • Glaze: pick a bowl, then tap the donut to dip it. Drizzle: drag across the donut. Toppings and Cream: tap or drag where they go.
  • The judge checks the glaze, and the amount and position of every topping, drizzle and dollop. A perfect copy earns ₹129.

1. Choose your car

2. Choose a circuit

  • Keyboard: W/A/S/D or arrow keys · Shift = DRS boost · R = reset onto the track.
  • Touch: ◀ ▶ on the left, BRAKE (and GAS) on the right, ⚡ boost, ↺ reset. With auto-accelerate on, you only steer and brake.
  • Corners: the faster you go, the wider the car turns. Brake before tight corners, or just hold the steering and the tyres scrub off speed for you.
  • Split-screen: P1 uses W/A/S/D + Shift, P2 uses the arrow keys + / for boost.
